Why open-source AI?

The best birding is where the signal isn't. Open models make that possible.

📡

Works with no signal

Canyons, ridgelines and forest trails don't have coverage. Nothing here needs it.

🔒

Your data stays yours

Recordings and locations never leave the device, which matters for sensitive nesting sites.

🎛️

Swap and tune

Change the model, the personality or the regional species list without asking a vendor.

🌱

Free to run

No per-request billing, so you can take it on every walk of the year.

What you need

A laptop, or a Raspberry Pi 5 for the pocket version, plus a microphone. Ollama runs the language model and BirdNET listens for birds.

Pick your voice

Small models like Gemma, Qwen or Llama run comfortably on modest hardware. Swap one for another and the whole personality changes.
